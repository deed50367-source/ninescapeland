# Design system

## Approved direction
Modern Enterprise Manufacturer: full-width product-led bands, white and cool-gray reading surfaces, restrained purple brand accents and orange inquiry actions. Existing content order, URLs, logo, imagery and functionality are preserved.

## Typography
Space Grotesk for headings; DM Sans for body; Noto Sans Arabic for RTL content. Fonts are served locally. Headings use balanced wrapping with zero letter spacing. Reading content stays around 75 characters per line.

## Tokens and components
Global CSS owns semantic color, shadow, radius and hero-overlay roles. The white background, cool muted surface, brand primary and inquiry accent correspond to the approved palette. Hero surfaces use a neutral charcoal overlay, reversed for RTL. Cards use 8px corners, thin neutral borders and restrained hover depth. Button variants separate inquiry actions, secondary outlines and quiet navigation.

## Motion and layout
Keep the existing sliding navigation highlight, dropdown and accordion interactions. No looping hero bokeh, shimmer, zoom or pulsing CTA. Respect reduced motion. Blog images and product renders retain stable aspect ratios. Admin tabs remain visible and scroll horizontally when necessary without changing permissions.

## Review
Representative public pages, localized pages, navigation and login rendering are browser-checked. Global styling reaches every route; page-specific content and authorization remain unchanged. Do not copy illustrative statistics or claims from the visual prototype.
