# Product

## Platform
web

## Users
Commercial playground buyers, venue operators and institutional procurement teams comparing equipment, projects and suppliers.

## Product Purpose
NinescapeLand presents commercial indoor playground, trampoline, ninja and soft-play equipment and receives customer inquiries.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve existing page addresses, navigation destinations, inquiry fields, analytics, legal content and SEO/GEO metadata. Public content supports English, Arabic, German, Spanish, Portuguese and French. Administrative modules remain permission-controlled.

## Brand Commitments
Preserve the existing logo, brand identity, business copy and site imagery. Do not introduce unsupported statistics, certifications or project claims.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing project galleries, product renders, articles and localized business copy. Reuse site images instead of substituting stock photography.

## Product Principles
Make products easy to inspect and compare. Keep inquiry entrances accessible. Visual changes must not change authorization or customer data.
